### DIRECTION-ECON - Autonomous Raider economy confirmed (2026-10-17)

- **Status:** Product direction recorded 2026-10-17 (documentation only; no runtime change).
- **Decision:** The user confirmed a Godville-style economy as game direction, not an experiment. The Raider sells, buys, equips, repairs, stashes, and departs autonomously in Desperanza through a deterministic, trait-weighted, server-owned utility policy. Weapons come mainly from raid loot and sometimes from seeded, finite, shared Desperanza trader stock. The Handler acts only through Signal actions, which may temporarily nudge weights but never pick items. AI models voice committed decisions and never make them. A model choosing among pre-ranked options is deferred and unapproved.
- **Supersedes:** G4/OUTFITTER Handler purchase/equip/repair/staging controls are transitional; do not extend them. G4 custody rules (unique ownership, move-once at admission, return unused/consume used, no duplicate spend) still apply to the Raider's own actions.

### Known limits and experimental assumptions

- All participants follow one scripted itinerary together. Co-location is real, but independently selected paths and incidental encounters are not implemented.
- `salvage-v1` dispositions, formula, timing, and stable-identity tiebreaks are demonstration rules, not validated fairness or balance.
- All surviving Raiders extract successfully in this slice; no damage, failure, or dangerous extraction exists yet.
- Extra contest recovery time has no environmental danger penalty.
- Names and the curated loot/search content are embedded selections, not a reusable content-loading pipeline. Source provenance is recorded in the README.
- No robot combat, backpack theft, optional Handler commands, equipment preparation, progression, public matching, or automatic redeployment.
- Raid state, credentials, and extracted results are in memory. Restart loses them; there is no production account inventory or recovery.
